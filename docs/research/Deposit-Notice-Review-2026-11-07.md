# Deposit, initial rent and notice: research decision

7 November 2026. Research-only follow-up; no application or database changes made for this request.

## Findings

The user's proposed separation of an agreed security deposit and the prorated initial rent is supported. A security deposit is not rental income or an automatically non-refundable onboarding fee. Rental Housing Act 50 of 1999, s. 5(3)(c), permits a landlord to require a deposit before occupation, in the amount specified in the agreement or otherwise agreed between the parties. R2,000 may be a condition of this particular offered rental; it is not a universal statutory amount or compulsory deposit for every rental.

Section 5(3)(d), read with the commenced 2007 amendment, addresses an interest-bearing account and the tenant's interest/proof rights. Keep deposits, accrued interest, lawful supported deductions and actual refund events separate from rent.

The rule 'give 30 days notice and automatically receive the entire deposit; otherwise lose it' is not justified. Section 5(5) provides at least one month's written notice for the periodic continuation it describes. This must not be expanded into a universal 30-calendar-day rule for every lease or an automatic first-of-month-only deadline. The agreement, rental arrangement and applicable law must be considered. Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 s. 14(2)(b)(i)(bb), where applicable to the fixed-term consumer agreement, provides 20 business days' recorded notice for early cancellation, subject to remaining liabilities and a reasonable cancellation penalty under s. 14(3). Its applicability must not be assumed for every private arrangement.

Section 5(3)(g) allows application of the deposit/interest to amounts the tenant is liable for under the lease, including reasonable repair/lost-key costs. Section 5(3)(h) requires supporting cost receipts. This is not blanket forfeiture: notice-related liability must be established and itemised, rather than treating the entire deposit as a penalty automatically. Proper notice alone also does not erase outstanding lawful liabilities.

Section 5(3)(i) provides deposit plus accrued interest within seven days after lease expiry where nothing is owed. Other conditions carry different deadlines: the baseline s. 5(3)(g) uses fourteen days from restoration of the dwelling for its deduction scenario, while s. 5(3)(k)-(m) addresses tenant non-response to an inspection request and a twenty-one-day deadline after expiry. Inspection failures can affect claims under s. 5(3)(j). Neither '30 days notice' nor '30 days after leaving' is a universal refund formula.

Notice records a proposed termination; it does not itself justify labelling rent as paid, stopping charges immediately or automatically using a deposit as last month's rent. Record notice date, requested termination date, contractual notice basis, applicable review and actual handover separately.

## Proposed application presentation (not yet implemented)

For an agreed R2,000 deposit and R1,500 monthly rent, with 15 January occupation, no previous payments and the selected actual-day proration policy:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Security deposit agreed | R2,000.00 |
| Deposit already received | R0.00 |
| Deposit remaining | R2,000.00 |
| Rent, 15–31 January (17/31 days) | R822.58 |
| Total initially due | R2,822.58 |
| Normal monthly rent from February | R1,500.00 |

Label the deposit as security held separately, refundable subject to lawful deductions and applicable rules. Save the actual agreed deposit and due date; do not hardcode R2,000 for all properties. An amount already received must not be demanded again. Deposit and rent balances/receipts remain distinct even if an overview displays their sum. A single bank transfer covering both purposes needs explicit allocation, not invented duplicate transfers.

Future notice workflow: record and acknowledge the actual notice and requested departure date, retain inspection/evidence, prepare an itemised deposit reconciliation including interest, and record the actual refund. Do not build an automatic all-deposit forfeiture switch or unconditional refund promise.
